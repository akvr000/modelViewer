AR Model Viewer

Upload your 3D model, then view it in your real space through your phone camera.

Upload a GLB file to see your model here
How it works
  • View in AR asks for camera access, then places the model on your floor or table and lets you walk around it. Needs Android Chrome with ARCore, or iPhone/iPad Safari.
  • Camera view works on any phone: it shows the live camera with your model floating on top. Drag to rotate, pinch to resize.
  • Camera access needs HTTPS. Opening the file directly from storage will not work. Host it (GitHub Pages, Netlify) and open that link on your phone.
